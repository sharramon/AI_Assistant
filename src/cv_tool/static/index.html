First Draft with Internal

When this tab opens, it ranks the JD's key terms and makes the draft proposals by itself. Tick what's true, then Apply. Terms are ranked by times in the JD, ×2 under required and ×1.5 under preferred qualifications, +3 if in the job title, and ×1.5 for a skill, tool or domain term. Untick a term in the table if it isn't a real keyword.

First Draft with Rezi

This writes a request with the JD and the CV text, puts the text on your clipboard too, and tells cv_agent at once: the click is your yes for the Rezi write, so it starts the First Draft by itself. cv_agent does it in Rezi, score only, and sends back the Rezi Score, the keywords and a PDF. They show up here, and the score is saved as the first round.
